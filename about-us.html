About us

Supplying the Building Trade Since 2010

Aegis Marketing Sdn Bhd was founded in 2010 and is led by a team with more than 16 years of experience in the building materials industry. We are a stockist of Skiezlight polycarbonate, aluminium composite panels (ACP) and roofing materials, and a partner of BlueScope Malaysia for metal deck roofing. We supply a wide range of roofing, cladding and decorative products to customers across Malaysia.

What We Supply

Eight Product Ranges for Roofing, Cladding and Decoration

Aluminium Composite Panel

Panels for awnings, wall cladding, signage and cabinets, in PE or PVDF coating.

Polycarbonate Sheet

Solid and hollow sheets for skylights and partitions, plus flat sheets for guards and signage.

Metal Deck

Visible fastener, concealed and insulated roofing, PU panels and custom accessories.

Corrugated Roofing

Clear polycarbonate, translucent FRP and corrosion resistant uPVC corrugated sheets.

Roof Truss

C-purlins, U-purlins, C-sections and battens in high tensile coated steel.

Acrylic Sheet

Clear, coloured, fluted, frosted and mirror sheets, cut to size.

Aluminium Decorative

Strip ceilings, gates, fluted panels and louvres in powder coat or wood grain.

Interior Decorative

PVC ceiling panels and PVC fluted wall panels that never need painting.
