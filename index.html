Common FAQ

Got questions? We’ve got answers

What products does Aegis Marketing supply?
Eight ranges: aluminium composite panels, polycarbonate sheets, metal deck roofing, corrugated roofing (polycarbonate, FRP and uPVC), roof trusses and purlins, acrylic sheets, aluminium decorative products (strip ceilings, gates, fluted panels and louvres) and PVC ceiling and wall panels. See all our products.
Who do you supply to?
Our customers include end users, hardware dealers, ironworks fabricators and awning installers, from one-off projects to regular trade supply.
Are different sizes, thicknesses and colours available?
Yes. Most of our sheets come in a range of sizes, thicknesses and colours. Share your requirements and we will recommend the best option for your application.
Do you provide delivery?
Yes, delivery can be arranged to your site or store. Contact us with your location and order details and we will advise on the arrangements.
How do I get a quotation?
Call, WhatsApp or email us with your specifications and quantities, and our team will get back to you promptly with pricing and availability.
Do your products come with a warranty?
Warranty coverage varies by product and manufacturer. For example, our metal deck roofing comes with a corrosion warranty of 10 to 20 years, depending on the sheet thickness. Ask our team about the material you need and we will walk you through the details.
Where are you located?
Our office and warehouse are at 15 & 17, Jalan Utama 1/2, Taman Perindustrian Puchong Utama, 47100 Puchong, Selangor.
