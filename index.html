Common FAQ

Got questions? We’ve got answers
What products does Aegis Marketing supply?
We supply solid, hollow and corrugated polycarbonate sheets, solid acrylic sheets, aluminium composite panels and metal roofing systems — all sourced for quality and consistency.
Who do you supply to?
Our customers include architects, engineers, hardware dealers, ironworks fabricators, awning installers and signage suppliers — from one-off projects to regular trade supply.
Are different sizes, thicknesses and colours available?
Yes. Most of our sheets come in a range of sizes, thicknesses and colours. Share your requirements and we will recommend the best option for your application.
Do you provide delivery?
Yes, delivery can be arranged to your site or store. Contact us with your location and order details and we will advise on the arrangements.
How do I get a quotation?
Call or email us with your specifications and quantities — our team will get back to you promptly with pricing and availability.
Do your products come with a warranty?
Warranty coverage varies by product and manufacturer. Ask our team about the specific material you need and we will walk you through the details.
Where are you located?
