Aluminium Strip Ceiling, Gates and Louvres

Aluminium decorative products are architectural finishes that combine good looks with long lasting performance: strip ceilings, louvres, fluted panels, cladding and gates.
On this pageStrip ceilingGate and fenceFluted panelLouvresFAQ

Because aluminium is lightweight, strong and does not rust, these products suit both indoor and outdoor use in Malaysia's hot, humid climate.

They come in a wide choice of profiles, colours and finishes, including powder coated, anodised and realistic wood grain. This lets architects, designers and property owners give homes, shops, offices and commercial buildings a clean, modern look. Aluminium is also fire resistant, low maintenance and fully recyclable, so these products are a practical and sustainable alternative to timber, PVC or steel.

Aluminium strip ceiling

Aluminium strip ceilings are made of long, slim panels that clip onto a hidden carrier system, giving a clean, seamless linear finish. They come in a crisp solid finish for a bright, modern look, or a wood grain finish that gives the warmth of timber without rot, termites or warping. Because they are lightweight, rust free and weather resistant, they are well suited to car porches, patios, entrances, corridors and building soffits, and they hold up to Malaysia's heat, rain and humidity.

Aluminium gate and fence

Our aluminium gates and fences are made from strong, hollow extruded profiles. They are light but sturdy, and they will not rust or corrode the way mild steel or wrought iron does. They come in two finishes:

  • Wood grain: the look of natural timber, in shades from light oak to dark walnut, without rot, termites or warping
  • Powder coating: a smooth, long lasting finish in colours such as white, black and maroon, resistant to fading, peeling and weathering

Gates can be made in swing, sliding, folding or auto gate designs. Profile sizes and patterns can be customised to match your home or building. They are built for Malaysia's hot, rainy climate and need very little maintenance, so your gate stays good looking and secure for years.

Aluminium fluted panel

Aluminium fluted panels have deep, evenly spaced grooves that give gazebo roofs, walls, columns, ceilings and facades a bold, textured, three dimensional look. Each panel is made from strong, lightweight extruded aluminium with interlocking edges, so the panels join neatly and install quickly with no visible joints.

Panels are available with or without PU foam insulation. The PU foam backing reduces heat and noise, which keeps indoor spaces cooler and quieter. For extra thermal performance, the foam can be supplied in a range of thicknesses to suit your project.

They are finished in durable powder coated colours, making them a long lasting, weatherproof alternative to timber or PVC fluted panels. They will not rot, warp or fade, and they will not attract termites. They are ideal for feature walls, shopfronts, reception areas, gate infill and building exteriors, where they add a modern, premium touch that needs very little maintenance.

Aluminium louvres

Aluminium louvres are rows of blades that control sunlight, airflow and privacy while giving a building a clean, modern architectural look. They are lightweight, strong and rust free, and come in powder coated colours or wood grain finishes. They are built to handle Malaysia's heat and rain with very little maintenance.

Sunshade louvres

Aluminium sunshade louvres are sleek horizontal blades fixed above windows, doors and facades to block direct sunlight and rain. Each blade has a curved front edge that gives a clean, streamlined look while adding strength and helping rainwater run off.

The louvres cut glare and heat build up indoors, which keeps rooms cooler and can lower air conditioning costs. They also protect windows and walls from the weather. Being lightweight and rust free, they are easy to install on brackets and need very little maintenance.

Z series louvres

Aluminium Z louvres are horizontal blades with a Z shaped profile that clip onto vertical carriers to form a continuous screen. Each blade overlaps the one below, so air passes through freely while rain and direct sunlight are kept out and views from outside are blocked. This makes them a good fit for fencing, boundary walls, privacy screens, M&E and aircon ledge covers, and building facades.

They come in three profile sizes: 70Z, 90Z and 140Z. The larger the blade, the greater the depth and coverage, so the right size depends on the scale and look of the project.

Baffle (box) louvres

Aluminium box louvres are hollow, rectangular blades mounted horizontally on vertical carriers. They are widely used for sun shading, facade screening, M&E and plant room enclosures, boundary walls and feature walls. They cut heat and glare while still letting air and natural light through.

The blades come in several profile sizes, so their depth and visual weight can match the design of the project. Installation is simple and neat.

Aerofoil louvres

Aluminium aerofoil louvres have smooth, wing shaped blades that clip onto a concealed carrier, for an elegant, flowing screen with a premium architectural finish. The curved, tapered profile deflects wind and sheds rainwater, and it filters sunlight while letting air flow freely. They suit facades, sun shading, feature ceilings, pergolas and walkway covers.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes are available for your aluminium decorative products?

Strip ceilings, fluted panels and louvres come in standard widths set by the profile you choose, and we cut the length to your project's requirements. Aluminium gate profiles come in a standard length of 6m.

Why choose aluminium over timber, steel or PVC?

Aluminium is lightweight, strong and does not rust. It will not rot, warp or attract termites like timber, and it lasts longer than PVC outdoors. It also needs far less maintenance than mild steel or wrought iron.

Which louvre type should I choose?

It depends on what you need it for and the look you want:

  • Z louvres: privacy fencing and screening
  • Aerofoil louvres: premium facades and ceilings
  • Box louvres: bold, geometric sun shading
  • Sunshade louvres: shading above windows
What finishes and colours are available?

We offer powder coated colours such as white, black, silver, bronze and maroon. Selected products also come in wood grain finishes that look like natural timber.
