Metal Roofing Sheet

Made from high tensile prepainted galvanised steel (PPGI), metal roofing sheet is a sustainable, long lasting and cost effective choice for many building projects.

We supply metal roofing sheets in a wide range of profiles and standard colours, in long continuous lengths. Fixed securely, a metal roof gives long service with very little maintenance in industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects.

A complete metal roofing system includes the roofing sheets, insulation and the structural components that hold everything in place.

Why metal roofing:

Long service life with minimal maintenance
Resistant to heat, rain and corrosion
Supplied in long continuous sheets
Wide choice of profiles and standard colours
Sustainable and cost effective

Used in:

Industrial buildings
Commercial buildings
Infrastructure projects
Roofing and wall cladding
